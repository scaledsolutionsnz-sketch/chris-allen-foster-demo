The person holding the moment together.

Chris Allen-Foster has been a registered celebrant in the Bay of Islands since 2013, when she completed her Certificate in Celebrant Studies through Celebrant School, Te Wananga Korowhai Aroha. She is a validated member of the Celebrants Association of New Zealand.

She works from Paihia and travels through Kerikeri, Kaikohe and the wider Northland. Every ceremony is written from scratch after a proper conversation, because a wedding built from a template sounds like one.

  • Registered to marry you, licence paperwork handled and lodged properly.
  • Written for you, no stock ceremony, no lines you have heard at someone else's wedding.
  • Weddings and farewells, the same steady presence at both ends of a life.
  • Happy to travel, beyond the Bay of Islands by arrangement.
Talk to Chris

How a ceremony comes together.

No mystery to it, and no pressure at any stage. Most couples are through the first three steps well before the invitations go out.

A proper chat

Coffee, a phone call or a video call. Chris listens to how you met and what you want the day to feel like.

A draft to read

She writes the ceremony and sends it over. Change anything, cut anything, add the bits only you would know.

Paperwork sorted

You apply for the marriage licence, Chris tells you exactly how and when, then lodges the documents afterwards.

The day itself

Chris arrives early, checks the sound, settles the nerves and leads the ceremony so you can be present for it.

A few recent ceremonies.

Beaches, paddocks, arches and the odd wharf. Every one of these was written for the people standing in it.
